Rise to Meet Me

Don’t reach to claim me.
Rise to meet me.

✦

I was never waiting to be found,
never a half in search of whole.
I built a home beneath my skin,
I learned the language of my soul.

Now you’re standing at the edge,
close enough to feel the fire.
But wanting me is not the same
as being worthy of desire.

✦

Don’t ask me to dim my light
so yours can burn brighter.
Don’t call it love if I must leave
myself outside the door.

I belong to me—

I can come close, I can let you in,
without surrendering everything.

I belong to me.
If I choose you, you’ll know what it means.
Don’t try to conquer what was born free.
Rise to meet me.

Bring me truth without possession.
Bring me strength without control.
